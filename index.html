01 · Foundation

Brand Story & The 3am Manifesto

Born from late-night pharmacy aisle frustration. Founded by Jeremy Adams and Jessica Biel to deliver clean, effective remedies without unnecessary additives.

The Late-Night Revelation

Origins

Like millions of parents, Jeremy Adams and Jessica Biel found themselves standing in the drugstore medicine aisle in the middle of the night, staring at children's remedies loaded with Red #40, Yellow #6, artificial sucralose, and chemical fillers.

When their children were ill, they wanted medicine that works without the junk. The status quo forced a compromise: either choose effective conventional medicine full of artificial additives, or unproven homeopathic remedies. KinderFarms was created to end that compromise.

The "Kids Deserve Less" Manifesto

Redesign 2026
"Less noise. Less clutter. Less fear. Less artificial. Clean, calm, effective care that lets parents breathe."

We don't shout over the medicine shelf. We calm it down. Every formula is physician-approved, backed by rigorous science, and formulated without artificial sweeteners or synthetic food dyes.

10 · Governance

Brand Rules, Dos & Don’ts

Strict design and communication guardrails to preserve monolithic brand equity across digital, physical packaging, and retail channels.

The DOs

  • Always use the unified monolithic KinderFarms® wordmark as the primary anchor.
  • Ensure clear space around the logotype equal to the height of the capital letter "K".
  • Highlight functional benefits (e.g., "0% Artificial Dyes", "WHO/AAP ORS") with clean pill badges.
  • Maintain high contrast ratios (minimum 4.5:1) for all clinical dosing copy.
  • Use photography featuring authentic, candid, warm parent-child interactions.

The DON'Ts

  • Never create new sub-brands or append standalone logos (e.g. No "KinderCare", "KinderPop").
  • Never alter the official brand colors or create gradient text across primary headlines.
  • Never use fear-based marketing, panic triggers, or alarmist imagery of screaming infants.
  • Never claim products are "100% chemical-free" (water is a chemical; accuracy matters).
  • Never compress, distort, or rotate the master wordmark.
